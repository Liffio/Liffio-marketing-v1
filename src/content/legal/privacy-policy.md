# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Liffio collects, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It covers our websites, the Liffio app, and the Instagram data we process through Meta's official Instagram API.

## 1. Who we are

Liffio is operated by Liffio Private Limited (CIN: U58200GJ2026PTC181271), a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at First Floor, Shreeji General Store, Sultanpura Naka Laheri Pura New Road, Vadodara - 390001, Gujarat, India ("Liffio", "we", "us", "our").

We play two different roles, depending on whose data it is:

- **Our customers' own data** (your account, billing, and how you use Liffio). We decide how this data is used. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we are the data fiduciary for it.
- **Our customers' audience data** (the people who comment on, message, or interact with the Instagram accounts our customers connect). We process this data only on behalf of, and on the instructions of, the customer who connected that account. The customer decides why it is processed. We act as their service provider: a data processor under the DPDP Act, and a Tech Provider under Meta's Platform Terms.

## 2. Who this policy covers

- **Customers:** people and businesses who create a Liffio account, including agencies and their team members.
- **Audience members:** people who comment on, message, or interact with an Instagram account that a customer has connected to Liffio. You may never have signed up for Liffio yourself. Section 5 explains how we handle your data.
- **Visitors:** people who visit liffio.com, or open a bio link page or short link that we host.
- **Creators Program applicants and members, and affiliates.**

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you give us

- Name, email address, and password, or your name, email address, and profile photo from Google if you sign in with Google
- The country you select for your account. We use it to decide your billing currency and your tax treatment. We do not infer your country from your IP address for this purpose.
- For Indian customers buying a paid plan, your state, which decides how GST applies. If you are registered for GST, your GSTIN and legal name for the invoice.
- Instagram username and profile link
- Billing details. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card number.
- Content you create in Liffio, such as automations, message templates, links, bio link pages, and posts you schedule
- Details of team members you add to your workspace
- Messages you send to our support team

### 3.2 Information we collect automatically

- IP address, and approximate location based on it. We use this for security, fraud prevention and rough analytics, not to decide your prices or your tax treatment.
- Browser, device type, and operating system
- Pages visited, features used, time on site, and interaction events
- Referral source and UTM parameters
- Time zone and language settings
- Error and performance logs

### 3.3 Instagram data from your connected account

You connect an Instagram Professional account by logging in with Instagram and approving the permissions Liffio asks for. We never ask for or store your Instagram password. Once connected, we access the following through Meta's official Instagram API:

| Data | Instagram permission | What we use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Account ID, username, name, profile photo, account type, and follower count | instagram_business_basic | To identify your connected account and show it in your dashboard |
| Your posts and reels, with their captions | instagram_business_basic | To let you choose which posts your automations run on |
| Comments on your posts and live videos, including the commenter's username, Instagram-scoped ID, and comment text | instagram_business_manage_comments | To run the comment automations you set up, including sending replies |
| Direct messages sent to your account and messages sent through Liffio, including the sender's username, Instagram-scoped ID, message content, and whether they follow your account | instagram_business_manage_messages | To run the DM automations you set up, show conversations and leads in your inbox, and apply follow-before-DM rules if you turn them on |
| Account and post insights, such as reach, impressions, and engagement | instagram_business_manage_insights | To show you analytics about your own account and content |
| Posts and media you schedule or publish through Liffio | instagram_business_content_publish | To publish content to your account when you tell us to |
| Access tokens | All of the above | To connect to your account securely. We never share tokens except with the service providers that host our systems. |

### 3.4 Our rules for Instagram data

We handle Instagram data under Meta's Platform Terms, and we commit to the following:

- We use Instagram data only to provide the features you set up and ask us to run.
- We do not sell, rent, or license Instagram data to anyone.
- We do not use Instagram data, even in aggregated or anonymised form, for Liffio's own marketing or advertising.
- **We do not send Liffio's own promotional messages to the people who contact you.** Automated messages carry only what you configured.
- We do not use Instagram data to build profiles of people, to decide anyone's eligibility for anything, to discriminate against anyone, or for surveillance.
- We do not use Instagram data to train AI models.
- We delete Instagram data as described in Section 8.

### 3.5 AI features (Lyra)

If you use Lyra, our AI assistant, we process the content you give it, and any of your Liffio or Instagram content you ask it to work with, to generate results for you. Lyra runs on AI models hosted on our own servers. We do not send this content to third-party AI companies, and we do not use it to train models.

If we ever move Lyra to an external AI provider, we will update this policy and tell you before the change takes effect.

### 3.6 Creators Program and affiliates

- **Creators Program applicants:** the details in your application form (name, email, country, Instagram username and profile link, follower range, content niche, and your answers). A member of our team also reviews your public Instagram profile.
- **Creators Program members:** we keep metrics about your connected account, including follower count, post count and how consistently you post, and we use them in an automated score that informs program decisions. Section 8.3 of the Creators Program Policy explains this in full, including what we do not use.
- **Affiliates:** referral activity, payout details, and country of residence. For Indian residents, your PAN and bank account details when we need them to pay you and deduct tax. To prevent fraud we also use IP addresses, device and payment-method fingerprints, and referral and payment patterns.

- Process payments, manage subscriptions, and issue tax invoices

Audience data is used only as described in Section 5.

## 5. If you interacted with a business that uses Liffio

If you commented on, messaged, or interacted with an Instagram account that uses Liffio, that business (our customer) decides how your data is used, and Liffio processes it on their behalf.

- **What we process:** your Instagram username and Instagram-scoped ID, the text of your comments and messages, whether you follow the business's account, and any details you choose to share in the conversation, such as an email address or phone number the business asks for.
- **Why:** only to run the automations the business has set up, such as replying to your comment or message, and to show the business its conversations and leads. We do not use your data to market Liffio to you.
- **Who is responsible:** the business is responsible for telling you how it uses your data. Questions about why a business messaged you, or what it does with your details, should go to that business first.
- **Deletion:** you can ask the business to delete your data, or email us at support@liffio.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", your Instagram username, and the business account involved. We will delete your data from Liffio within 30 days and let the business know.

**Bio link pages and short links:** when you open a bio link page or short link that we host, we record the visit on our servers (time, referring site, device type, and approximate location based on IP address). We use this to show the page owner basic click analytics. See our Cookie Policy for cookies.

## 6. Legal basis for processing

**India (DPDP Act).** We process personal data either with your consent, which we ask for when you sign up and which you can withdraw at any time, or for a legitimate use the DPDP Act allows. For example, when you voluntarily give us data for a specific purpose, such as a support request, or when the law requires us to process it, such as keeping tax records. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened, and some features may stop working.

**EU and UK (GDPR).** If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on: performing our contract with you; our legitimate interests in securing and improving Liffio and preventing fraud, balanced against your rights; your consent, for marketing emails and non-essential cookies; and our legal obligations.

**Audience data.** The customer who connected the Instagram account is responsible for having a lawful basis to process their audience's data.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- **Access:** get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it
- **Correction:** fix inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion:** ask us to erase your data (see Section 10)
- **Portability:** where applicable, get your data in a machine-readable format
- **Withdraw consent:** at any time, including for marketing emails
- **Grievance redressal:** raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (see Section 15)
- **Nomination:** name another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to
- **Complain to a regulator:** in India, to the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process first. In the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email support@liffio.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We respond within 30 days.

If you are an audience member, you can use these rights with the business you interacted with, or with us.

## 10. Deleting your data

- **Delete your Liffio account:** email support@liffio.com from your account email with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your registered name. We delete your data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law (see Section 8).
- **Disconnect Instagram:** disconnect your Instagram account inside Liffio, or remove Liffio's access from your Instagram settings under apps and websites. Either way we stop accessing your account immediately, delete your automations, leads, messages and link data straight away, and remove your access token and account identifiers within 30 days.
- **Audience members:** see Section 5.

## 11. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption of stored access tokens, access controls, and restricted access to our production systems. No system is 100% secure. If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities, including the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

**Reporting a security issue:** if you find a vulnerability in Liffio, email support@liffio.com with the subject "Security Report". We review every report.
