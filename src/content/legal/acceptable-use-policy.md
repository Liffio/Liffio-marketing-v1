# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules for using Liffio. It keeps Liffio safe and compliant for all our customers, and for the people who receive their messages. It is part of our Terms and Conditions.

## 1. Prohibited content

You must not use Liffio to send, publish, link to, or promote content that:

- Is illegal under Indian law or the law of the recipient's country
- Breaks Instagram's Community Guidelines or Meta's Community Standards
- Promotes violence, hate speech, or discrimination
- Contains sexually explicit material or pornography
- Infringes anyone's intellectual property
- Promotes illegal drugs, weapons, or regulated goods without the required authorisation
- Contains malware, viruses, or phishing links
- Is a scam, or misleads people about what they will get

## 2. Messaging rules

Meta has strict rules for automated messages on Instagram. Liffio is built to follow them, and you must follow them too.

**2.1 Only message people who contacted you first.** Automations may only message someone who commented on your post or live video, sent you a message, or replied to your story. A like, a follow, or a view does not give you permission to message someone.

**2.2 Respect Meta's time windows.** You can reply to a message within 24 hours of the person's last message. You can send one private reply to a comment, within 7 days of that comment. Liffio enforces the one-reply-per-comment rule for you. Once a window closes, automations must not message that person again until they contact you.

**2.3 No broadcasts.** Do not send bulk messages to lists, past commenters, or followers who have not contacted you within the window.

**2.4 The Human Agent tag is for humans only.** Meta's Human Agent tag lets a real person on your team reply by hand up to 7 days after someone's last message. Liffio does not currently offer this, so nothing in the product uses it today. When we do offer it, it must never be used for automated messages, sequences, or promotions, and this rule will apply from the day it ships.

**2.5 Keep your promises.** If your post says "comment a keyword to get something", your automation must send that thing.

**2.6 Respect "stop".** If someone asks you to stop messaging them, stop.

**2.7 Don't collect sensitive data in DMs.** Never use automations or lead forms to ask for passwords, card or bank account numbers, government ID numbers such as Aadhaar or passport numbers, or health information.

**2.8 Don't target children.** Do not use Liffio to target people you know are under 18, or to collect their personal data.

## 3. Prohibited activities

When using Liffio, you must not:

- **Spam:** send unsolicited or bulk messages, or any message that breaks Section 2
- **Harass:** use automations to harass, stalk, threaten, or intimidate anyone
- **Deceive or impersonate:** scam or mislead people, or pretend to be another person or brand
- **Connect accounts without permission:** connect an Instagram account you don't own or aren't authorised to manage
- **Misuse audience data:** sell, share, or use audience data in any way our Terms (Section 4.3) or the Meta Platform Terms prohibit
- **Abuse our systems:** bypass plan or rate limits, scrape data without permission, reverse engineer Liffio, or load test or stress test our infrastructure
- **Get around Meta's rules:** use Liffio to bypass Meta's API restrictions, or combine Liffio with unofficial tools that log in with your Instagram password
- **Make false claims about Meta:** say that Liffio, or your use of it, is endorsed by, partnered with, or certified by Meta or Instagram. You can say Liffio works with Instagram's official API.

## 4. How sending works on Liffio

Liffio sends messages only through Meta's official Instagram API, and it applies sending limits for you. You don't need to add delays or limit your sends by hand.

What you control is quality:

- Make automated messages useful and relevant to what the person asked for
- Be clear about who you are and why you are messaging
- Make it easy for people to reply to a real person or ask you to stop

## 5. Monitoring and enforcement

We may review how Liffio is used, including through automated checks, to find breaches of this AUP. If we find a breach, we may:

- Warn you
- Pause specific automations
- Suspend your account
- Terminate your account without refund

We will also suspend or end access where Meta requires it.

We share information with authorities only when the law requires it, such as under a court order or a valid government request.

## 6. Reporting abuse

Anyone can report misuse of Liffio, including people who received unwanted messages from a business that uses Liffio.

Email support@liffio.com with the subject "Abuse Report" and include the Instagram username of the account that messaged you, screenshots, and dates. We review every report.

## 7. Copyright and trademark complaints

If content on a bio link page, short link, or anything else we host infringes your copyright or trademark, email support@liffio.com with the subject "IP Complaint" and include:

- Your name and contact details
- The work or trademark you own
- A link to the content you are complaining about
- A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised
- A statement that your complaint is accurate and that you are the owner, or authorised to act for the owner

We review complaints within 5 business days and remove or disable access to infringing content where appropriate. If we remove your content, you can reply with a counter-notice explaining why it does not infringe.

We may update this AUP from time to time. If we make a material change, we will tell you by email or in the app.
