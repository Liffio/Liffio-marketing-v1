# Refund Policy

**Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]**

Thank you for choosing Liffio. Please read this policy before you subscribe. It explains when you can get a refund or a credit, and how cancellation works.

## 1. Try Liffio free first

Liffio has a Free plan, so you can try the platform before you pay. We encourage you to use it to decide whether Liffio is right for you before buying a paid plan.

## 2. General rule: payments are non-refundable

Liffio gives you immediate access to digital tools, so payments are generally non-refundable. This includes:

- Monthly and annual subscription fees
- Renewal payments that have already been processed
- The unused part of a billing period

Sections 3 and 4 explain the exceptions.

## 3. When you always get a refund

- **Billing error:** you were charged the wrong amount because of an error on our side.
- **Duplicate payment:** you were charged twice for the same subscription on the same workspace.

In both cases, we refund the extra amount in full. For Indian customers we issue a GST credit note with the refund.

## 4. When you get a credit or refund for service problems

- **Major technical failure:** if a Liffio bug stops you from using core features for more than 48 hours in a row and we can't fix it, we will give you a pro-rated credit for the time lost, or a refund for that time if you would rather cancel.
- **Loss of Instagram access on Meta's side:** if Meta limits or suspends Liffio's access to Instagram features your plan depends on for more than 48 hours in a row, we will give you a pro-rated credit for the time those features were unavailable.
- **A Liffio error affecting your Instagram account:** if Meta restricts your Instagram account because of a Liffio error, for example Liffio sent messages you did not set up, tell us. We will investigate, and where we find a Liffio error, we will give you a credit or a refund.

## 5. Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from your dashboard, or by emailing support@liffio.com from your account email with the subject "Cancel subscription".

Cancelling stops your next renewal. You keep access until the end of your current billing period. We do not give partial refunds for the rest of that period.

## 6. Instagram account actions

Liffio works only through Meta's official Instagram API, and our automations follow Meta's messaging rules. Meta alone decides how it enforces its rules on Instagram accounts, and we don't control that. Apart from the cases in Section 4, we do not give refunds if Meta limits or suspends your Instagram account, for example because of your content or how you use automations.

## 7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We can usually fix billing problems faster than a bank dispute.

If you open a chargeback without contacting us first, we may suspend your account while the dispute is reviewed. If the chargeback turns out to be unjustified, we may close your account and block the email address and payment method used from signing up again.

## 8. How to request a refund or credit

Email support@liffio.com with:

- Your account email
- Your transaction ID or receipt
- The reason for your request
- Evidence of the technical issue, if that's the reason

We reply within 5-7 business days. Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it. Credits are added to your Liffio account.

## 9. Contact

For refund questions, email support@liffio.com.
