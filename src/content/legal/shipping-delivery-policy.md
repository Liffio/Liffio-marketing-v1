# Shipping and Delivery Policy

**Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]**

Liffio is an online software service. We don't sell or ship physical products.

## 1. How your plan is delivered

When your payment is successful, your plan is activated on your Liffio account straight away, and we email a receipt to your account email. For Indian customers we also issue a GST tax invoice, which you can download from your account. You use Liffio in your web browser at app.liffio.com.

## 2. If your plan isn't active

If your plan isn't active within 24 hours of a successful payment, email support@liffio.com with your account email and transaction ID. We will activate it or refund the payment.

## 3. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellations and refunds are covered by our Refund Policy.
