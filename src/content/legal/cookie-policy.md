# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]**

This Cookie Policy explains how Liffio uses cookies and similar technologies on liffio.com, app.liffio.com, and the bio link pages and short links we host.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your computer or phone. Similar technologies include browser local storage and session storage. In this policy, we call them all "cookies".

## 2. Cookies we use

### 2.1 Essential cookies (always on)

These are needed for Liffio to work. You can't switch them off in our settings.

| Cookie | Where | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Login and security | app.liffio.com | Keeps you signed in and protects your account and session | Until you log out or your session expires |
| Your cookie choices | liffio.com | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies | 12 months |
| Payment | Checkout | Set by Razorpay during checkout to process your payment and prevent fraud | Set by Razorpay (see their privacy policy) |

### 2.2 Referral cookies (non-essential)

We use these for our Affiliate Program, to credit the person who referred you to Liffio.

| Cookie | Where | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ref` | liffio.com | Remembers which affiliate link or referral code brought you to Liffio | 90 days |
| Referral backup (local storage and session storage) | liffio.com | Keeps a backup copy of the same referral code | Local storage until you clear it; session storage until you close the tab |

Where the law requires your consent for these (for example, if you are in the EU or UK), we only set them after you agree. If you don't agree, you can still enter a referral code when you sign up and it will work the same way.

### 2.3 Analytics

We use Umami to understand how our website and app are used. Umami does not place cookies or store identifiers in your browser, and does not collect personal data.

### 2.4 Advertising

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, such as the Meta Pixel. If we add them, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## 3. Bio link pages and short links

When someone opens a bio link page or short link that we host, we record the visit on our servers: the time, the referring site, the device type, and an approximate location based on IP address. We use this to give the page owner click analytics. These pages do not use advertising cookies.

## 4. How to control cookies

- **Our cookie settings:** where we ask for your consent, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies, and change your choice at any time from the cookie settings on our website.
- **Your browser:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you won't be able to log in to Liffio.

## 5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when the cookies we use change, or for legal or operational reasons. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

For questions about cookies, email support@liffio.com.
