# Affiliate Program Policy

**Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]**

This Affiliate Program Policy ("Policy") governs the Liffio Affiliate Program. You accept it when you join the program in your dashboard, and it sits alongside our Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy.

If you signed up through someone else's link or code, you don't need to accept this Policy.

## 1. Program overview

The Affiliate Program lets any registered Liffio user, including users on the Free plan, earn commission by referring new paying customers. There is no application. Your affiliate link is in your dashboard, and you join the program by accepting this Policy there.

Key features:

- 50% lifetime recurring commission on the Net Revenue of every payment a referred workspace makes
- 90-day attribution window
- 15-day grace period if a referred subscription lapses
- 20-day hold before earnings can be withdrawn
- On-demand withdrawals once your available balance reaches $50
- 10% discount for referred users on their first payment
- Custom referral codes alongside your auto-generated code

## 2. Commission

### 2.1 Rate and duration

You earn 50% of the Net Revenue from every payment a referred workspace makes, for as long as that workspace keeps an unbroken active paid subscription. There is no time limit and no cap on total earnings.

Commission on a workspace ends permanently if its subscription lapses beyond the grace period in Section 4.

### 2.2 What "Net Revenue" means

Net Revenue is the amount Liffio actually keeps from a payment, after deducting:

- GST and other taxes. Prices shown in rupees include GST, so the tax is inside the amount charged and comes out before commission is calculated.
- Payment processing fees charged by our payment provider
- Any discounts or credits applied to the payment
- Refunds and chargebacks

Your dashboard shows the gross payment and the Net Revenue used for each commission, so you can always see how a figure was reached.

### 2.3 First payment discount for referred users

Users who sign up through a referral link, or enter a valid referral code at signup, get 10% off their first payment only. The discount is applied automatically and does not repeat. Because commission is calculated on Net Revenue, your commission on that first payment is based on the discounted amount.

### 2.4 Per-workspace commission

Commission is calculated per workspace. If a referred user buys several workspaces within the 90-day attribution window, each workspace earns its own commission stream independently. A lapse on one workspace does not affect the others.

### 2.5 Annual plans

Annual subscriptions are sold on the Starter, Growth and Business plans. Commission on an annual payment is 50% of its Net Revenue, credited to you in 12 equal monthly instalments across the year rather than as a single payment.

Each instalment enters the 20-day hold when it is credited. If the annual payment is refunded or charged back, or the subscription ends, instalments that have not yet been credited stop, and credited ones are clawed back under Section 7.

### 2.6 Eligible plans

Commission applies to all paid plans, billed in rupees or US dollars. Free plan signups and Creators Program access don't earn commission, because no payment is made.

### 2.7 Example (illustrative only)

A referred user subscribes to Business at ₹2,499/month, a price that includes GST. After GST and the payment processing fee, Liffio's Net Revenue on that payment is roughly ₹2,040, so your commission is roughly ₹1,020. The exact figure depends on the tax treatment and the fee charged, and your dashboard shows both.

This is an example, not a promise of earnings.

## 3. Attribution

### 3.1 Attribution window

Commission applies to workspace subscriptions the referred user buys within 90 days of creating their account. Purchases after the 90-day window don't earn commission.

### 3.2 First referral wins

The first affiliate link clicked, or the first referral code entered, gets the credit. Later links or codes from other affiliates don't override it.

### 3.3 Attribution is tied to the user account

Attribution is tied to the referred user's account, not to individual workspaces. You don't need separate links for each workspace they create.

## 4. Grace period for missed renewals

### 4.1 Continuous subscription

Lifetime recurring commission is earned only while each referred workspace keeps an unbroken, active subscription. Commission is workspace-specific.

### 4.2 The 15-day grace period

When a referred workspace's subscription expires or fails to renew, a 15-day grace period starts on the expiry date.

- **Renewed within 15 days:** the subscription counts as continuous and commission continues.
- **Not renewed within 15 days:** you permanently lose commission on that workspace, even if the user subscribes again later.

### 4.3 New workspaces during a grace period

New workspaces the referred user buys during a grace period, and within the 90-day attribution window, earn commission as normal. The grace period only affects the workspace that expired.

**Example:** a referred user has Workspace A (Business) and Workspace B (Starter). Workspace A expires on June 1, so its grace period ends on June 16.

- **User renews Workspace A on June 10:** within the grace period. Commission continues on both workspaces.
- **User renews Workspace A on June 20:** after the grace period. You lose commission on Workspace A permanently. Workspace B is not affected.
- **User buys Workspace C on June 8:** Workspace C earns commission as normal. Workspace A is still lost if it's renewed after June 16.

### 4.4 Permanent loss

Once commission on a workspace is lost:

- The loss can't be reversed, even on appeal
- We'll email you when a grace period ends without renewal
- Commission you already earned from that workspace, whether pending or available, is not affected

### 4.5 Timing

The grace period starts on the subscription expiry date. You can track your referred workspaces in your affiliate dashboard. We'll try to email you a day before a grace period ends, but we can't guarantee it.

## 5. Tracking

### 5.1 How we track referrals

When someone clicks your link, we try several methods to record the referral, and use the first one that works:

1. A first-party referral cookie set by our server (lasts 90 days)
2. The `?ref=` code in the link, recorded on our server when the page loads
3. A backup cookie set in the browser
4. Session storage, for the same browsing session
5. Local storage, as a last backup (Safari and iOS may clear it quickly)

Where the law requires consent for cookies and browser storage, for example for visitors in the EU or UK, we only use those methods after the visitor agrees. Referral codes entered at signup always work.

### 5.2 Link formats

Every affiliate gets an auto-generated referral code when they register:

- Standard link: `liffio.com/?ref=x7k2mQ9p`
- Short link: `liffio.com/r/x7k2mQ9p`
- Custom code link: `liffio.com/?ref=yourcustomcode` (if you set one)

Your auto-generated code and your custom code both work at the same time.

### 5.3 Custom codes

You can create one custom referral code:

- Letters and numbers only, no spaces or symbols
- 3 to 20 characters
- Must be unique across Liffio
- Works in links and in the referral code box at signup
- Takes effect straight away
- You can change it at any time. Your old custom code stops working immediately.

### 5.4 Entering a code at signup

Users who enter a valid referral code at signup receive the same 10% first payment discount, and earn you the same commission, as users who click your link.

## 6. Payouts

### 6.1 Hold period

All earned commission is held for 20 days before it becomes available to withdraw. This protects against refunds and fraud. Held commission doesn't count toward your withdrawable balance.

### 6.2 On-demand withdrawals

There are no automatic payouts. You can request a withdrawal at any time once your available balance reaches $50.

### 6.3 Minimum payout

You need at least $50 of available (not held) earnings to withdraw. If your balance is negative because of clawbacks, it must be cleared to zero before any withdrawal.

### 6.4 Payout stages

- **Scheduled:** an annual-plan instalment not yet credited
- **Pending:** credited, within the 20-day hold
- **Available:** hold complete, ready to withdraw
- **Withdrawal requested:** you've asked for a payout
- **Approved:** we've approved it and are processing it
- **Paid:** funds sent to your payout method

### 6.5 Processing time

Once approved, funds are sent within 5-10 business days. Timing can vary by payment method and country.

## 7. Refund and chargeback clawback

If a referred user gets a refund, or a payment is reversed through a chargeback or payment dispute, the related commission is reversed:

- If the commission is still pending or available, we deduct it directly
- If it has already been paid out, we deduct it from your next available balance
- If your balance isn't enough, your account carries a negative balance that must be cleared before future withdrawals
- For annual plans, instalments that have not yet been credited are cancelled

Repeatedly referring users who request refunds or dispute payments may lead to a review or suspension of your affiliate account.

## 8. Tax and identity verification

### 8.1 Affiliates resident in India

- You must give us your PAN, and we verify your bank account, before your first payout.
- We deduct tax at source as Indian tax law requires at the time of payment, and we provide the certificates the law requires. We will tell you the rate and threshold that apply to you before your first payout.
- If you are registered for GST, tell us before your first payout so we can handle invoicing correctly.

Payouts are held until we have the documents we need. We track your cumulative payouts by Indian financial year (1 April to 31 March) and will email you as you approach a verification threshold.

### 8.2 Affiliates outside India

You must give us a valid payout method, confirm your country of residence, and, if we ask, sign a declaration that you are not resident in India. We may also ask for tax documents that Indian law requires for payments abroad, such as a tax residency certificate.

### 8.3 Your tax responsibility

You are responsible for reporting your affiliate income and paying any taxes due in your country of residence. We are not responsible for your personal tax obligations beyond the deductions Indian law requires us to make.

## 9. Fraud prevention

### 9.1 Prohibited activities

The following are strictly prohibited and lead to immediate suspension of your affiliate account and loss of all pending commission:

- Self-referrals, including referring your own accounts or devices
- Sharing your commission with the person you referred, or any similar arrangement
- Paying or incentivising people to sign up through your link
- Creating fake accounts to generate commission
- Using misleading, deceptive, or false advertising
- Cookie stuffing or any other artificial attribution
- Working with referred users to generate commission through signups and refunds
- Creating multiple accounts to avoid fraud checks

### 9.2 Risk scoring

We check fraud risk on each transaction using signals such as device and payment-method fingerprints, referral speed, payment behaviour, and account activity. IP addresses are stored for fraud detection only and are not used to decide attribution. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle this data.

### 9.3 Transaction outcomes

Based on risk scoring, a transaction may be:

- **Approved:** commission enters the normal 20-day hold
- **Held for review:** commission is held longer while we review it manually
- **Rejected:** commission is cancelled and the account is flagged for monitoring

We may withhold, cancel, or claw back any commission we find was obtained through fraudulent, deceptive, or manipulative means.

## 10. Rules for sharing your link

- **Where you can share:** your website, social media, YouTube, email newsletter, and direct conversations.
- **Automated Instagram replies:** you may send your affiliate link in an automated DM only when the person asked for it, for example by commenting a keyword on your post or messaging you, and only within the messaging rules in our Acceptable Use Policy. Never send it in unsolicited DMs, bulk messages, or emails to people who didn't ask for it.
- **Disclosure:** clearly say that your link is an affiliate link wherever the law or the platform requires it, including ASCI guidelines in India, FTC rules in the US, and Meta's branded content policies.
- **No claims about Meta:** don't say Liffio is endorsed by, partnered with, or certified by Meta or Instagram, or call Liffio an official Meta partner. You can say Liffio works with Instagram's official API.
- **No impersonation:** don't present yourself as a Liffio partner, employee, or representative.
- **No brand bidding:** don't bid on Liffio's brand keywords in paid ads without our written permission.
- **No guaranteed earnings:** don't present projected affiliate earnings, including figures from any Liffio calculator, as guaranteed or expected income.

## 11. Changes to the program and termination

We may change, pause, or end the Affiliate Program at any time, including commission rates, attribution windows, grace periods, and payout thresholds.

If a change reduces your commission rate, shortens a grace period, or ends recurring commission on existing referrals, we'll email active affiliates at least 30 days before it takes effect. Changes that improve your benefits take effect immediately.

We may suspend or terminate any affiliate account for breaking this Policy, for fraud, or for any other reason at our discretion. If termination is for a policy breach or fraud, commission still in the hold period may be forfeited.

## 12. Moving from the previous version of this Policy

If you joined the program under an earlier version of this Policy, we will email you at least 30 days before this version applies to your existing referrals. Until then, those referrals are treated under the earlier terms.

The commission rate and lifetime recurring structure are unchanged from the previous version. What has changed is that commission is calculated on Net Revenue rather than the gross payment, that annual-plan commission is credited in monthly instalments, and that chargebacks are clawed back alongside refunds.
